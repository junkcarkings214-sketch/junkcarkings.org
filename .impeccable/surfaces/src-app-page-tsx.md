# Home — surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Scope: all public surfaces; `/` is the primary artifact, and
`/sell-your-car`, `/contact`, and the 404 inherit the world. Admin is untouched.

Audience: a Central/North Texas owner standing next to a dead or unwanted
vehicle, on a phone, in daylight. Job: hand over the vehicle's details and a way
to reach them, believing a person will read it. Proof: the review is manual, the
evaluation is free, the offer carries no obligation. Constraint: no invented
claims, no prices, no testimonials; the logo and its palette are the only fixed
brand assets.

Direction: **The Warm Porch**, replacing The Notice Pole at the owner's request
("human friendly, not robotic, trustworthy, pleasant to the eye, modern but not
sharp"). Answers recorded: soft and warm; Bricolage Grotesque for headlines; the
junkyard photograph as a full-bleed hero with a navy wash. This is a user-pinned
direction, so the direction roll did not choose it: the roll dealt seven
unrelated worlds (Googie diner, anime command wall, darkroom, BBS, origami, film
strip), none of which carry a trusted local buyer. The pin beats the roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A neighbour's front porch, not a poster on a pole. The page is calm,
rounded and warm, so a worried owner feels looked after, and it refuses the
sharp, stamped, condensed-caps look of the flyer wall and the generic white SaaS
hero with a three-card band.

OWN-WORLD: Warm sand and linen surfaces (no pure white panels), deep navy ink for
type and the brand's dark sections, gold used as a warm highlight only on the
single action and small icon accents, soft rounded corners at 20px, gentle
diffuse shadows with a soft blur and a small offset, never hard offset blocks.
Headlines in Bricolage Grotesque with its optical size axis, body in Figtree,
generous line height, comfortable measure.

STORY: The car in the driveway is a problem someone can hand to a friendly
local person. I say what it is, a person reads it, and they call me back with an
offer I can say no to.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The junkyard photograph fills the hero edge to edge with a navy
wash that is strongest behind the words. On the left, a large soft headline
("Turn your unwanted car into an offer."), one warm supporting sentence about a real
person calling back, and a single gold primary action "Get my offer" with a
quiet secondary phone link. Below the headline, three small reassurance marks
(free evaluation, no obligation, a real person answers). The year/make/model
offer panel is the first thing below the hero, overlapping the photograph's
bottom edge in a soft rounded sheet.

FORM: The Warm Porch: a full-bleed photographic hero with rounded soft sheets,
user-pinned in place of the roll. seed: 333e2745b660a23a (roll dealt; user pin beat it)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
